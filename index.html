About us

We are a student chapter for people interested in how information is created, organized, shared, and used. We welcome students in Library and Information Science (LIS) and anyone curious about the technology behind it.

Our mission

To connect UAlbany students with the people, ideas, and tools shaping information science and technology. We do this through networking mixers, hands-on workshops, panel discussions, site visits, and other events that build skills and community.

Our vision

A community where every LIS student sees the full range of paths the field offers, from libraries and archives to data, user experience, and information technology, and feels ready to pursue them.
